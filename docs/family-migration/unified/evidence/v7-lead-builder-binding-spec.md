## Decisions (DEC-5 resolved by lead builder under the Architect directive)
- r08-v6 is preserved as the compatible transitional two-family contract (revocation assurance + terminal revocation). It is NOT repurposed and none of its files or records change.
- r08-v7 is the unified successor, CONDITIONAL on final version-allocation review. It is built on ONE reusable engine. v7 carries exactly ONE family now: irreversibility. (anchor_deadline_to_inputs, ObservationPolicy.max_age_seconds and ObservationPolicy.clock_tolerance_seconds are BLOCKED, see decision records; do NOT register families for them.)
- Envelope protocol version is separate from family schema revision: a ContractProfile has `version` ("r08-v7") and every family row has its own `family_schema_revision` (int, v7 irreversibility = 1). The family schema revision is part of the effective-policy preimage.
- No general policy interpreter, no executable rules, no mutable globals. Families are typed, registered in an immutable table, each with its own schema, validator, caller normalizer and composer.
