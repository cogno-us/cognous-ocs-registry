## 1. Constitution terms that govern how statuses are read (pinned 1c2e920f)

The Constitution has no glossary entry for "adopted" or "frozen". The rules below are the ones it actually states.

**Adopted / canonical standing**
- EP2 (ENGINEERING_CONSTITUTION.md): proposals, branches, local results and recommendations "do not acquire canonical standing merely by existing or appearing correct". The system must identify "the authorized, traceable transition by which a candidate becomes accepted". "Technical integration, formal qualification, organizational adoption and deployment are distinct transitions."
- CA19: "only competent authority confers canonical standing"; "Treat local success, generated output or merge mechanics as organizational adoption" is the prohibited behaviour.
- ADOPTION.md "Standing": publication, a copy, a passing check or "a repository merge does not by itself establish adoption". An adopting authority "must identify an exact revision, the scope and effective point of adoption". Step 4: "A technical merge cannot stand in for this decision." Step 6: each context "makes or records its own revision selection".
- Consequence for the ledger: ADOPTED_FROZEN needs a decision record by a competent authority (operator decision, architect ruling accepted by operator, owner acceptance) that names scope. A merged PR, a doc saying "adopted" with no source, or an architect ruling "pending operator adoption" is not enough.

**Frozen**
- Used only as "frozen contracts" (ADOPTION.md step 6: a new text "does not silently amend existing frozen contracts") and "frozen expectations" (qualification oracles). A canonical decision "remains operative until lawfully superseded"; reopening needs "an identified authority, controlling source, reasons, scope, version and qualification implications". "A change in current operational status does not rewrite a frozen record."
- Consequence: freeze is a recorded act over a stated scope. A doc that says "not a frozen schema" or "names are frozen at the M0 gate" is NOT a freeze. Qualification's expectations are frozen by Qualification, not by the hub (CA04; hub qualification-contract.md).

**Qualified / independently qualified / independent**
- CA04: "Distinguish implementation inspection, independent qualification, competent certification and final integration." Independent expectations are "recorded before candidate acceptance"; "a qualifier cannot adjust frozen expected results to make a candidate pass"; "Producer-supplied expected outputs are proposals, not independent certification."
- "Developer suites and static analysis are not independent qualification." "Source inspection is review, not execution." "Integration is not deployment, release or production qualification." "Planning completion does not establish executable readiness or authorization."
- Results transfer "only to the exact sources, profiles and conditions actually evaluated"; a mismatch against frozen expectations is FAIL; unresolved mapping or unavailable evidence is BLOCKED or NOT ASSESSED.
- ADOPTION.md "Independent qualifier": assesses "against independently specified obligations"; "Independence cannot be inferred from a second automated process alone"; "Required independence may not be waived through role renaming or delegation". EP7/CA23: a passing test "cannot certify itself"; human presence is not enough without authority, evidence, independence and accountability.
- Lifecycle states to keep distinct (CA16): proposed, documented, implemented, developer-tested, independently qualified, integrated, deployed, production-qualified. No silent promotion between them.
- Mapping to the six lane statuses: PROPOSED_ONLY = proposed/documented; IMPLEMENTED_NOT_QUALIFIED = implemented/developer-tested/integrated without independent qualification; ADOPTED_FROZEN = competent-authority adoption (and freeze) recorded; no lane status means "independently qualified".

Use its terminology as the pinned reference; do not claim adoption.
