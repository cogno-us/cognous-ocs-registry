## 2. Frozen demonstration scope and limits
**MSOP-F1a-C11** (operator-approved G0, PR #40): one synthetic tenant and one governed scope, local non-effecting emulator, Linux x86-64 CPython 3.11–3.13, synthetic Ed25519 keys. The `f1ab` envelope has **five mandatory sets**: units, adapters, actions, institutions, authority domains. The first three are demonstrably narrowed; institutions and authority domains are present and carried unchanged. F4/F5 and Control Plane EXT-S3-1..3 are deferred unless a source-backed minimum-scenario requirement reopens them by operator-approved amendment.

Example acceptance pair: caller permits `u-read` and `u-write`, authenticated selected policy permits `u-read` only; `u-read` remains allowed and `u-write` is refused. A forged or missing policy cannot create permission. Scope is a slice of C11's F1a assertions, **not full C11 qualification**. Do not alter `engine/safe_executor.py` (protected blob `2fc880dae37eac76e5074533435740ce0684209d`). No live/production effects, cloud, real key ceremony, new families, other tenants, broad integration platforms or universal policy interpreter.

## 3. Two concrete milestones

### M1 — WORKING REGISTRY (developer demonstration, non-effecting)
A real `r08-v2` selection is parsed, authenticated and bound to an actual `f1ab` record using the **existing Registry verification/composition path**. A bounded **Runtime-owned narrowing adapter** consumes the composed five-set effective policy through the **existing deterministic local-policy evaluator**, with no modifications to its protected implementation. The non-effecting harness runs the allowed/refused pair and records source/selection/effective-policy identity. **No fabricated verification results, no bypass, no raw unverified record routed into execution, no grants/authorization/emulator effects and no claim of commit-time safety.** If the existing Runtime evaluator cannot be exercised without a new effect path, use a pure/inspection-only harness and report the boundary; do not create an unsafe backdoor.

M1 exit is objective: same caller and exact runtime evaluator; changing only the authenticated selection changes `u-write` from caller-allowed to policy-refused; `u-read` allowed; malformed/forged/missing required input refuses; effective-policy digest matches the applied five-set value; existing unaffected behavior is compared against a pinned baseline. Raw test evidence and limitation statement required. M1 is **not security-qualified integration**, and must never be promoted as such.

### M2 — GOVERNED INTEGRATION (effect-time and durable authority)
The Control Plane carries a complete, exact canonical binding in a successor approval/claim under its existing commitment, while no-binding claims remain byte-identical. The Runtime holds durable selection/activation/revocation state and revalidates the claim, active policy, generation, ratchet and changed conditions under the existing SQLite `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` effect-commit boundary. End-to-end emulator demonstrates allow/refuse, withdrawal after approval, stale/replayed claims, restart/restore, broken anchor and evidence fault behavior. Independent review and qualification follow at exact pins. **Never carry an M1-only unverified selection or pre-claim check into effecting operations.**

## 5. Mandatory controls and program execution
- The **first new builder session works on V01 only**, and only after separate explicit implementation authorization. Its first action is exact-head source inventory and mapping of a **pure/side-effect-free** harness into the existing evaluator, not another architecture review. If M1 would require an unsafe authority bypass, STOP with a bounded blocker and the minimum safe alternative.
- Document each sprint's authorization, exact plan/Constitution/ADR pins, files, predeclared oracles, raw tests, baseline deltas, reviewer, limitations and exit. No hidden phase advancement, no blanket authorization, no autonomous merge unless expressly permitted. A fresh lead-builder session per sprint; short-lived reviewers; GitHub PR/status/decisions are the continuity record.
- The pinned Control Plane baseline includes reported **35 AGT failures** (1,192 pass/90 skip/35 fail); verify the comparable baseline at V02 and retain failure identity and scope. No new failures may be concealed or dismissed. S01 Registry regression reported 6,079 pass/2 skip, not independently qualified.
- Maintain `docs/sprint-status.md` as the single live status. Preserve v1.4 and Amendment 1, G0, S01, historical contracts and evidence. No edits to historical plans.
- No new normative `policy_binding_digest` sibling, grant authority, claim reason code or policy interpreter. Preserve R6 `policy-binding/0.1` distinct from the canonical `ocs-policy-binding/1` and enforce their adopted shared identity. Refuse undeclared fields on legacy claims. The binding's exact named schema, not a disputed count, is authoritative.
- Statless Registry snapshot floors are not durable activation. `verify_and_decide_v2` lacks floor inputs; V04/V05 must enforce durable authority/floor semantics at the consumer. Legacy v1 hand-built-object behavior remains historical and is not a valid v2 isolation boundary. Preserve v1 acceptance/refusal behavior.
- Signed selection, approved claim and active Runtime state are separate. Registry does not authorize effects. No old/latest selection fallback. Unknown evidence/authority, stale generations, lineage replacement or ratchet downgrade fails closed. No network I/O under the Runtime effect transaction; source-owned code safety invariants remain mandatory.
- Independent cryptographic reviewer and Tier 1 lead must be identified before independent oracle freeze, with no AI-as-independent qualification. CCTV vector-license status remains open; synthetic keys never imply operational trust. External-anchor rollback window limitation remains disclosed.
