## Recommendation

**Next bounded increment (S07 candidate): extend the existing registry pattern to `allowed_institutions` and `allowed_authority_domains`, the two remaining allow-set fields of `LocalExecutionPolicy`.**

Why this one: it is the only candidate that reuses the proven contract, consumer and equivalence machinery almost unchanged, needs no new baseline, and keeps the narrowing-only guarantee provable by the same exhaustive differential method. It is the smallest step that tests whether the pattern generalizes before touching a different evaluator.

Why not the others now: F6 needs a new baseline and a different record shape; F4 and F5 are wide, number- and structure-heavy, and depend on unresolved classifications (F4: W6-042 and `60` versus `300`; F5: W8-014). Ceilings and prefixes within F1 need different semantics and should follow separately.

Risk stated plainly: institution and authority-domain checks are admission to an effect, so a Tier 1 independent qualification owner is still needed, and the S05 limits (builder-authored oracle, Copilot review only) would carry over. Nothing here approves a pin or any activation.

Preconditions for any such sprint, each needing a separate operator decision: authorization of the sprint; a decision on the contract version change; whether a Tier 1 independent qualification owner is designated; resolution or acceptance of the S06 A questions (deny-all expression, refusal semantics). **This is a recommendation. It is not authorization, and S07 is not started.**
